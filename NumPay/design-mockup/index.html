Palette

Deep violet-black #0A0912 base, warm over the current blue-black. Primary accent shifts slightly to #7C6DF0 for better contrast on dark. Success green and pending amber added.

Typography

Interface in Geist (sans). All digits — number, balances, amounts — in Geist Mono with tabular figures so nothing shifts as values change.

Layout

Desktop: persistent sidebar nav + main + right rail with QR & frequent contacts. Mobile: floating glass tab bar with a raised Send FAB at center. Same components reflow between them.

Interaction

Send is a 3-step sheet: number → amount → review. Recipient resolves the moment 11 digits are entered — you see who you're paying before you commit.

Safety

Hold-to-send instead of tap. Small UX friction at the exact point of irreversibility. Resolved recipient card uses a green outline to confirm the match is real.

What's new vs today

Adds balance, activity feed, QR receive, frequent contacts. Removes the flat tab bar. Register becomes onboarding, Lookup merges into Send.
