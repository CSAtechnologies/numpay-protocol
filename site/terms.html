3. Transactions are final

Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed once confirmed. You are responsible for checking the recipient, the amount, the asset and the network before you confirm. Sending to a wrong or incompatible address will usually mean the funds are lost, and we cannot retrieve them.

4. NumPay IDs

A NumPay ID is a short number that maps to wallet addresses you control, so that people can pay you by number instead of by address. Registering an ID publishes that mapping so it can be resolved by others.

  • An ID points at addresses. It does not hold funds and it is not a bank account.
  • You must not register an ID in order to impersonate another person, business or brand, and we may remove any mapping that is used to deceive.
  • Availability of a particular number is not guaranteed, and registration depends on the underlying network confirming it.
