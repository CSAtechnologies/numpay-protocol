07Security & ownership

Non-custodial,
by design.

NumPay is a self-custody wallet. Your private keys are generated and stored only on your device. We never hold, see, or control your funds. Your NumPay ID is just a friendly pointer to the addresses you already own.

You hold the keys

A standard recovery phrase you control. Move to any compatible wallet anytime, with no lock-in and no permission needed.

Send-time checks

Verified-ID and anti-poisoning warnings before funds ever leave.

On-device encryption

Biometric unlock and encrypted local storage keep your wallet sealed.
